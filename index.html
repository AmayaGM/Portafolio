INSIGHTS & ANALYSIS

  • Double-Entry Redundancy: 100% operational redundancy from logbook + PC data entry.
  • Registration Latency: 55-100 seconds per user, causing bottlenecks.
  • Peak Hour Bottlenecks: Queues of up to 8 users during 6-9 PM, delaying 30% of entries.
  • Administrative Burden: Staff spends 42 min/day (4.5 hrs/week) on manual transcription and data digitization.
  • Financial Voucher Vulnerability: Increased risk of reconciliation errors and billing disputes from accumulated payments.
